# SAWII PERSONA — the book

*A companion made of three minds — and one that ages.*

Owner: Anas AlSawy · Status: living document · This is the canonical description of the idea.

---

## Why this book exists

The idea lived for months across chat logs, branches, half-written notes, and code scattered
across repositories. It worked, but it was **scattered** — folders here, files there, the *why*
buried under the *how*. This book fixes that. It is the single place where the whole thing is
written down: the idea, the minds, the aging, every feature, every open question.

If something here ever conflicts with an older note, **this book wins.** When the idea grows,
the book grows with it.

---

## The one-line version

> An LLM today is a read-only projector: it answers, but nothing it lives through changes it.
> Sawii Persona is three minds running at once — one speaks, one checks and remembers, one watches
> how you feel — and, at its heart, a design that lets experience **write back into the model**, so
> the companion **ages** the way a person does: not decay, but accumulation.

## The spirit (do not lose this)

The owner's instruction, kept verbatim because everything else answers to it:

> *"I want to mimic how humans get affected by events … weights get affected by events but they
> usually do not update … my design is very simple: we're just adding an updater and adding the
> verifier into the loop and letting changes take effect naturally. We're not forcing it, we're not
> trying to make it happen, or trying to prevent it from happening."*

> *"I want to see things emerge … being upset one day, moody because of something that happened
> yesterday or the week before, anxious because it knows tomorrow's deadline. Continuity. Longevity.
> Aging. It's another way of dealing with AI … it's very intimate, and it's profoundly amazing …
> naturally, naturally, naturally — only."*
