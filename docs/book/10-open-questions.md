# 10 · Open questions

The decisions that are **not yet made.** Each is the owner's call. This chapter exists so nothing is
decided silently — a design this important should be decided on purpose.

## 1. "The world" — does the mind need an outside?

A person is shaped not only by their own judgment but by **the world**: consequences, other people, a
body that gets tired. A mind that learns only from its own verifier is a **closed loop** — if the
verifier drifts, the drift becomes invisible to itself.

> Should the mind be able to drift **anywhere its life takes it**, fully and freely?
> Or should there be **something like what a person has** — a piece of the world it cannot forge?

This is **not a filter** and not a safety mechanism. It is a question about reality. It is the one
unresolved piece of the aging design.

## 2. The re-architecture — does C keep the psych read?

When verification moves to C, does C also keep reading the person (chord, hypotheses, readings)? The
proposal is **yes** — reading the person and checking the output are the same "critical faculty," one
coherent mind. To be confirmed.

## 3. The re-architecture — what model does each mind run?

The remap **forces a model reassignment.** Today C runs a *psych* model and B runs the strong
*reasoner*. After the swap, the verifier (C) needs a reasoner, and B (the hands) needs a
coding-capable agent. The exact model for each mind is to be decided and tuned.

## 4. What ages?

Just **A** — the voice, the one that talks to you? Or does the aging extend to **all three minds** —
their behaviour drifting from experience too? The book assumes A, but this is open.

## 5. Whose experience ages it — per persona, or shared?

Each companion aging from **its own** person (separate biographies), or a shared base that ages from
everyone? This one matters enormously for how the aging is built, especially with many personas.

## 6. The hands — how is Cline run?

A **persistent Node sidecar** (warm sessions, no per-task startup tax — recommended) or a **CLI
subprocess** per task (simpler)? And which provider should the hands use?

## 7. Live, or batch-first?

The destination is the live updater. The recommendation is **batch-first** — it proves the signal
with zero risk and builds the journal and anchor that the live version needs anyway. To be confirmed
by the owner.

---

*These are the owner's decisions. When one is made, it moves out of this chapter and into the design.*
